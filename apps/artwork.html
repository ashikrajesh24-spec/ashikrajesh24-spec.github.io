App 13 · Website & security

Client Artwork Portal

Each client brand gets a private gallery of its approved artwork on the company website, protected by email one-time codes.

  • A gallery page for each brand at /artwork/<brand>
  • Codes sent only to the brand's own email domains; that client sees only their brand
  • Revocable manager passkeys
  • 10-minute codes, idle lock, per-email and per-IP rate limits
  • Every login and view logged
